Real-time Problem Solving

Colling Terplant

The Problem: Traditional cooling systems can be expensive, bulky, and require complex refrigeration mechanisms. We wanted to explore a compact cooling solution using the thermoelectric (Peltier) effect.

The Solution: Built a compact thermoelectric cooling system using a Peltier module. When DC electricity is supplied, the Peltier module creates a hot side and a cold side. A copper plate, heatsink, exhaust fan, and DC motor are used to remove heat from the hot side, allowing the cold side to remain cool. Water placed on the cold side can be cooled for an extended period and can eventually freeze into ice.

Components: Peltier Module, Copper Plate, AC-to-DC Converter, DC Motor, Heatsink, Exhaust Fan.
